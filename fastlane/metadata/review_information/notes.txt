Bark Off Dog plays a looping animal sound to discourage a barking dog. No account and no login.

To review: open the app and tap Dinosaur, Tiger, or Lion. The sound loops until you tap the same button again. Record uses the microphone and stays on the device.

The app is not a safety device. The listing says to call 999 in danger.

Privacy policy: https://bark-off-dog.pages.dev/privacy-policy.html
