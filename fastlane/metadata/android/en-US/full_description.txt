Bark Off Dog is a dinosaur in your pocket. A dog comes at you, it loops a loud sound and invites the dog to rethink its choices.

• Tap Dinosaur, Tiger, or Lion
• Record your own sound if you can out-roar a dinosaur
• Volume all the way up, phone speaker on
• It still plays when the ringer is off

Call 999 if you are in danger. This app can shout. It cannot save you.

This has not been scientifically proven. If a dog still attacks you, we take no responsibility.
