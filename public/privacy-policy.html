Bark Off Dog

Privacy policy · 25 September 2026

English

Bark Off Dog plays a looping sound on your phone. There is no account.

If you record your own sound, the recording stays on your device. The iOS and Android apps save it in the app’s private storage. The website keeps it only for that browser session. Bark Off Dog does not upload recordings, and it does not use analytics or advertising.

The microphone is used only when you choose Record. You can deny that permission and still play the bundled sounds.

The dinosaur, tiger, and lion sounds are part of the app. Tiger and lion recordings are used under Creative Commons licenses, credited in the store listing.

Questions: thcathy@gmail.com

简体中文

「不要吠」在你的手机上循环播放声音。没有账户。

你录下的声音只留在自己的设备上。iOS 和 Android 会把它存在应用自己的存储空间里。网页版只留在那一次浏览器会话里，关掉就没有了。这个应用不会上传录音，也没有分析或广告。

麦克风只在你点「录音」时使用。不允许麦克风，仍然可以播放内置声音。

恐龙、老虎和狮子的声音是应用的一部分。老虎和狮子录音使用知识共享许可，商店说明里有出处。
